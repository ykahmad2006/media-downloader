Yours to keep, or free to share

Some things are yours to keep.Others aren’t.

Fetchly is for videos and photos you made yourself, things shared under a Creative Commons or public-domain licence, and anything the owner has said you can download.

Most of what is on the big platforms belongs to someone else. Saving it without permission can break the site’s rules and copyright law. If you aren’t sure, ask the owner first. You’re responsible for what you download. Read more on rights and privacy.
