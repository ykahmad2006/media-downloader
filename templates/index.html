Yours to keep, or free to share

Some videos are yours to keep.Others aren’t.

Fetchly is for videos you made yourself, videos shared under a Creative Commons or public-domain licence, and anything the owner has said you can download.

Most videos on the big platforms belong to someone else. Saving them without permission can break the site’s rules and copyright law. If you aren’t sure, ask the owner first. You’re responsible for what you download.

Common questions

My link didn’t work. Why?

The video may be private, deleted, age-restricted, or on a site we can’t reach. Check that it plays in your browser while you’re logged out, then try again.

Why only these four sites?

They’re the ones we test and keep working. Links from other sites are turned away on purpose, which also keeps the service safe to run.

Why did my Instagram story fail?

Stories are only visible to logged-in viewers, and we never ask for your login. Posts, reels and carousels that anyone can open work fine.

Is there a size limit?

Yes. Files bigger than 1 GB are turned away so the site stays quick for everyone.

Do you keep my files?

No. Each file is deleted right after you download it, and we never ask for your name or email.
