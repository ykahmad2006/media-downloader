Rights and privacy

What Fetchly does with your links and files, and what you may save with it.

What you may save

Fetchly is for videos, photos and pictures you made yourself, things shared under a licence that allows it, and anything the owner has said you may keep. Most things on the big platforms belong to someone else, and saving them without permission can break a site’s rules and copyright law. You are responsible for what you download and what you do with it.

What we handle

  • Links you paste. We read the public page to list what can be saved. The link and the options we found are kept in memory for up to an hour, then dropped.
  • Photos you upload for “Photo with the tweet across it” are used only to make your picture. They are not shared or kept for any other purpose.
  • Files we make for you are deleted a few minutes after you download them, and unused files are deleted within an hour.
  • No accounts. We never ask for your name, email or a login.

Logs and security

To keep the service running and stop abuse, our web server may record your IP address and the time of each request, and a limit on how many downloads one visitor can run is kept in memory. We do not sell or share this.

Cookies and analytics

Fetchly sets no cookies by default and uses no advertising. If the site owner turns on analytics, a notice asks first, and nothing loads until you choose Allow. Your choice is remembered in your browser only. Fonts are served from this site, not from a third party.

Pictures and emoji

Emoji in tweet pictures are drawn with Twemoji (CC-BY 4.0), fetched from a public code host and cached. Profile pictures and tweets are fetched from the platform at your request and are not stored beyond the download time above.
